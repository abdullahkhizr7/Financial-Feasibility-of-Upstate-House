Financial Feasibility of Upstate House, LLC
M.Sc. Banking & Financial Analytics dissertation modeling a 328-unit affordable housing development in NYC, financed under HPD's New Construction Finance (NCF) programme with 4% LIHTC equity and SONYMA MIF-enhanced tax-exempt bonds.
Core question: Once built, will rental income cover operating costs and debt service — sustainably — over 30 years?

Finding
The capital stack ($248.7M) exactly matches TDC with no funding gap. DSCR and I/E ratio stay above regulatory minimums in every year of the 30-year projection — the project is feasible, but structurally dependent on simultaneous LIHTC + HPD NCF subsidy access.
